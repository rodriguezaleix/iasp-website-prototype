Who we are &
What we do

The global network
  • A worldwide membership based association founded in 1984.
  • An independent, non-profit, non-governmental organisation.
  • A Board of Directors ensuring a true international identity.
Driven by experts
  • Managed by experts in science and technology parks, and innovation districts.
  • Multilingual management team with World HQ in Malaga (Spain) and Beijing (China).
Empowering ecosystems
  • Enhance new business opportunities for members and their companies.
  • Increase the visibility of our members and multiply global connections.
  • Represent areas of innovation at international forums and institutions.
