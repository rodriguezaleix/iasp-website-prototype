About IASP

IASP in a few words

Connecting the professionals managing innovation ecosystems worldwide to drive growth, internationalisation and effectiveness.

Our mission is to be the global network for science parks and areas of innovation, and to drive growth, internationalisation and effectiveness for our members.

What we do

  • Coordinate an active network of managers of science/technology/research parks, innovation districts and other areas of innovation.
  • Enhance new business opportunities for members and their companies.
  • Increase the visibility of our members and multiply their global connections.
  • Represent parks and areas of innovation at international forums and institutions.
  • Assist the development of new parks and areas of innovation.

Who we are

  • A worldwide membership based association founded in 1984.
  • An independent, non-profit, non-governmental organisation.
  • Managed by experts in science and technology parks, innovation districts, areas of innovation and other innovation spaces.
  • A Board of Directors ensuring a true international identity (max 2 voting directors per country).
  • A multilingual management team with World HQ in Malaga (Spain) and a branch in Beijing (China).

The IASP forest

As part of our commitment to sustainability and the UN SDGs, we collaborate with Treedom, a web platform for remote tree planting, making the world greener one tree at a time.

We have already started an IASP forest of cacao trees in Cameroon, and IASP members get their very own tree to add to it.
